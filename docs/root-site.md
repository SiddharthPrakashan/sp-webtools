### Instagram Followers Check

**Discover who doesn't follow you back and review your pending Instagram follow requests.**

Have you ever wondered which Instagram accounts you follow but don't follow you back? Or how many follow requests you've sent are still pending?

My first web tool, **Instagram Followers Check**, helps you understand your Instagram connections by analyzing data you've downloaded from Instagram.

**What can you do?**

- **Find Non-Followers:** See which accounts you follow that don't follow you back.
- **Find Accounts You Don't Follow Back:** Identify followers you're not following.
- **Check Pending Follow Requests:** Review follow requests you've sent that haven't been accepted, according to your exported data.
- **Protect Your Privacy:** Analyze your files locally without uploading them to our servers.

**Why use it?**

- Free to use.
- No Instagram login or password required.
- No connection to your Instagram account.
- Browser-based file processing.
- Simple and convenient to use.

### [Try Instagram Followers Check →](https://webtools.siddharthprakashan.com/ig-followers-check/)

*The tool analyzes your exported Instagram data and does not provide live Instagram account information. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.*

## The Ideas Behind My Work

### Privacy Is a Priority

I believe people should have control over their personal data. Wherever practical, I build tools that process information directly on the user's device rather than requiring unnecessary server uploads.

### Technology Should Be Accessible

Useful technology should be available to everyone, regardless of technical experience, background, ability, or financial circumstances.

I aim to build intuitive, inclusive digital experiences that remove barriers instead of creating them.

### Learning, Sharing & Collaboration

Software development is a continuous learning journey. I value open-source technology, constructive feedback, and the contributions of people who share their knowledge and ideas.

### Respect for Nature

I believe technological progress should go hand in hand with environmental responsibility. I value lightweight, efficient applications and thoughtful use of digital resources, while recognizing that sustainability requires continuous improvement.
